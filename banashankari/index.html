Frequently Asked Questions

When should I consult a gynaecologist?

+
You should consult a gynaecologist if you experience irregular periods, severe pelvic pain, unusual discharge, or as part of your routine annual wellness screening.

What are common symptoms of ovarian cysts?

+
Common symptoms include pelvic pain during periods, abdominal bloating or swelling, and pain during intercourse.

What causes irregular periods?

+
Irregular periods can be caused by hormonal imbalances, PCOS, thyroid disorders, extreme stress, or sudden weight fluctuations.

When should pelvic pain be checked?

+
Pelvic pain that is severe, persistent, or interferes with your daily activities should be evaluated by a gynaecologist immediately.

When should heavy bleeding be evaluated?

+
If you soak through one or more sanitary pads every hour for several consecutive hours, or experience periods lasting longer than 7 days, you should consult a doctor.

How often should women have a gynaecology check-up?

+
It is recommended for women to visit a gynaecologist at least once a year for a routine preventive health screening.

Can PCOS cause irregular periods?

+
Yes, Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is one of the most common causes of irregular or missed menstrual cycles.

When should I consult a doctor about menopause symptoms?

+
You should consult a specialist if severe hot flashes, mood swings, or night sweats start disrupting your daily sleep and quality of life.
